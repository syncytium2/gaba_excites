## The task

names "the running record of decisions and why", and in AGENTS.md's "Rules that are load-bearing". The log is good at

**Extract the decisions already taken into `docs/adr/`.** Do not invent new decisions, and do not change any. Each ADR
records something the log shows was decided, with its date and reasons, and links back to the log entry.

## The format: the estate's, as bugarach uses it

Tony already reads this shape. Bugarach borrowed it from colonel_kernel, which has run it for some fifty decisions. Read
`~/Developer/bugarach/docs/adr/README.md` and two or three of its ADRs before writing. Do not link to sibling repos from
this public repo: a relative path out of this tree resolves for nobody but this machine. Mention them by name.

- **One decision per file:** `docs/adr/NNNN-<the decision as a short sentence, kebab-case>.md`, zero-padded and
  incrementing. Titles state the decision, for example `0002-measurements-are-on-true-vm-not-the-recorded-trace.md`.
- **The Nygard template:** a `# ADR-NNNN: <title>` heading, then Status, Context, Decision, Consequences. Optional
  sections are fine when they earn their place ("What this does not decide").
- **Status:** `Proposed`, `Accepted`, `Deprecated`, or `Superseded by ADR-NNNN`. For these, write "Accepted, <date the
  log shows it was decided>".
- **Accepted ADRs are immutable.** A later change is a new ADR that supersedes the old one. Amend in place only when no
  decision changes: a dated pointer, a correction.
- **`docs/adr/README.md`** holds the convention and an index table (ADR, title, status). Say there how ADRs relate to
  `docs/process.md`, which stays the narrative, and to AGENTS.md, which stays the operational rules. When an ADR and
  AGENTS.md concern the same rule, cross-reference them, so they never disagree.

## Rules

  `docs/process.md`, except to add a single dated entry saying the ADRs were extracted. Do not touch any code.
- **Quote the reasons the log gives.** Do not supply better-sounding ones. Where the log records the owner's words
  ("hold off on changing this", "do not adjust the slider when ion channel properties are changed"), keep them.
- **House style:** "data" is plural; American English; name things instead of numbering them in prose. No private paths
  or student data: this repo is public.
- **Worktree:** `git worktree add ../gaba_excites-worktrees/adrs -b adrs`. Never `git add -A`. The other two sessions may
  be committing to main. Rebase onto main before you merge, and expect `docs/process.md` to have gained entries.
- **No deploy is needed;** the ADRs are not served. Ask Tony before pushing.
- **Before finishing,** offer Tony a murderboard pass (`~/Developer/murderboard`) on the ADRs, to check each claim
  against its log entry.
