# DeFazio et al. 2002 — E_GABA in GnRH neurons

DeFazio RA, Heger S, Ojeda SR, Moenter SM (2002) Activation of A-type
γ-aminobutyric acid receptors excites gonadotropin-releasing hormone neurons.
*Mol Endocrinol* 16:2872–2891. doi:10.1210/me.2002-0163.

## The number

> E_GABA measured 100 msec after GABA application was −36.5 ± 1.2 mV
> (n = 16 cells from 13 adult diestrous females).

Read from the publisher's full text on 2026-09-21. Gramicidin perforated
patch, which leaves intracellular chloride as the cell keeps it. From the
abstract: E_GABA is "depolarized relative to the resting potential", and
"rapid GABA application elicited action potentials in GnRH neurons".

## Where it is used

The GnRH model's `defaults.eGaba` in `src/core/models.ts`, carried by every
GnRH preset in `src/core/protocol.ts`. Adjustable in the app like any other
reversal.

## A note on the date

The owner asked for this value as "from the 2000 paper". PubMed has no 2000
paper from this group reporting it; this 2002 paper does, word for word.
