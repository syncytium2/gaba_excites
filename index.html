gaba_excites

A current-clamp excitability simulator that runs in the browser. A single-compartment cortical pyramidal cell — the regular-spiking model of Pospischil et al. (2008), with Traub-type sodium and delayed-rectifier potassium currents and a slow M-type potassium current for spike-frequency adaptation — recorded in current clamp.

  • Set the passive properties the way they are read off a rig: input resistance, capacitance, series resistance (with bridge balance and pipette capacitance) and holding current.
  • Run a family of current steps, or any list of steps, and read off rheobase, spike threshold, action-potential shape, measured input resistance and the F–I curve. Pin a curve, change something, and compare.
  • Add conductance-based glutamate and GABA PSCs — rate, peak conductance, rise and decay — and move the GABA reversal potential to see the same inhibitory conductance hyperpolarize, shunt, or excite.

The model is ported from its ModelDB source (123623) and checked spike-for-spike against NEURON running the original mechanisms. The model and every equation are on the Methods page, which needs no JavaScript. Source: github.com/syncytium2/gaba_excites (MIT).
